The visualiser rebuilt as a tested C++20 project, in the original look.

**Download:** `PathfindingVisualizer_v2.0.0_windows.zip` (Windows 10/11: unzip and run `pathfinder-app.exe`) or `PathfindingVisualizer_v2.0.0_linux-x86_64.tar.gz` (x86-64 Linux with X11). SFML is linked in; nothing else to install.

### What's new since v1.0
- **Weighted grids and weighted A\*.** Cells can cost 1–9 to enter (mud). An A* weight slider trades optimality for speed, with a proven bound: the path costs at most w times the cheapest. On weighted terrain, w = 5 expands 140× fewer cells than Dijkstra for a 13.7% costlier path.
- **Compare All:** runs the five algorithms on the grid on screen and lists their cost and cells visited.
- **Search and Grid tabs:** replay speed, pause, skip, wavefront shading by visit order; random walls, perfect mazes, weighted terrain; size, wall-density and seed sliders; wall, mud and erase tools. The window can be resized and the UI no longer freezes during a search.
- **A search library** (`include/pathfinder`) with no graphics in it: each search returns its path, cost and expansion order, which the app replays.
- **15 Catch2 property tests** on seeded random grids, a headless benchmark (`pathfinder-bench`), and CI on GCC, Clang, MSVC and AddressSanitizer/UBSan.
- **Fixed:** the crashes when running without a start or end, and the frozen window during a search.

Full details and benchmark results are in the [README](https://github.com/anson10/Path-Finding-Visualiser#readme).
